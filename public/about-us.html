OUR STORY

Caring for Your Skin Since Day One

Facial R Us was founded with a single vision: to make professional skincare accessible to everyone. What started as a small clinic in Malolos has grown into a trusted multi-branch facial and skincare company operating in Malolos, Pulilan, and Malabon. We believe that every person deserves to feel confident in their own skin, and we are committed to delivering personalized treatments using premium, dermatologist-approved products.
